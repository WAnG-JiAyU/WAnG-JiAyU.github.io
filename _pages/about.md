Hello, I am a postdoctoral researcher at <a href='https://liphy.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/en'> Liphy (Interdisciplinary Laboratory of Physics)</a> at <a href='https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/'>Université Grenoble Alpes</a>.
I am always curious about how things work in nature, and I'd love to explain them by science.

Previously, I obtained my PhD from the <a href='http://www.dalembert.upmc.fr/ijlrda/'>l'Institut Jean Le Rond d'Alembert</a>, <a href='https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/'>Sorbonne University</a>, France.
My PhD research focuses on elasto-capillary interactions, exploring how elasticity and capillarity interplay in mechanical systems. Additionally, I study structural mechanics, analyzing instabilities and nonlinear behaviors. 
<!-- You can find my thesis <a href='/assets/pdf/PhDThesis_Manuscript_JiayuWang.pdf'>here</a>.-->
My methods are deeply connected to optimization, stability analysis, and bifurcation theory. 

My primary focus is on theoretical modeling and numerical analysis. I write my own code with <a href='https://web.casadi.org'>CasADi</a> in Python. You can find some interesting videos of my simulations in the Gallery part.
