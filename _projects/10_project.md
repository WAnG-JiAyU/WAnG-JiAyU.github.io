The cable-actuated bar is made by an elastic bar with rigid cable-guides fixed on one surface.
An inextensible cable passing through the support holes of each cable-guide. By pulling the cable, one deforms the elastic bar.

In the simplest system: a straight beam with controlling cable passing through holes of identical distance to the bar, the bar deforms into spiral shape due to the presence of friction.
