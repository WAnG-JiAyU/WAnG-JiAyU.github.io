This simulation is inspired by the work of <a href="https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/alexia-beale">Alexia Beale</a> on the swelling of chickpeas and hydrogel beads.

The simulation is coded as a constrained optimization problem with <a href='https://web.casadi.org'>CasADi</a>.
